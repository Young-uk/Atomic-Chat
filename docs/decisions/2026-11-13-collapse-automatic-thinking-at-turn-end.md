# Collapse automatic thinking previews at turn end

Keep a live thinking preview open through later answer or tool phases of the same assistant turn, then collapse it when the turn finishes, fails or is cancelled. Track enclosing-turn activity separately from each reasoning phase so ending a thought does not prematurely hide it. Explicit reader open/close choices always take precedence. Historical thoughts remain collapsed by default and can be reopened.

This supersedes the completion-height latch in the live working timeline decision. Preserve recorded block order, reasoning timers and inference/cache behavior. Validate completion geometry, reader overrides and tail anchoring in Chromium across supported widths, fonts and themes.
